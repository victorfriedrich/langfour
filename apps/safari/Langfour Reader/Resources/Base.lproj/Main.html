Langfour Reader

Read any article in Safari as clean text. Tap a word to translate it and save it to your flashcards.

Turn it on in Safari

  1. 1 Open any website in Safari.
  2. 2 Tap the page menu next to the address ( or aA), then Manage Extensions.
  3. 3 Turn on Langfour Reader and tap Done.

Or: Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions → Langfour Reader.

Then read

  1. 4 On an article, open the page menu again and choose Langfour. The article opens as clean text.
  2. 5 Sign in once from Aa, with the code from the email. Then tap a word to translate it, or select a sentence. Safari’s Back button takes you back.
